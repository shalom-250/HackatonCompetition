# Job Seeker Node: User Guide 👤

## 1. Identity Injection (`/jobs/[id]/apply`)
Inject your profile into a mission node. Use the 4-phase high-fidelity form:

- **Phase 1: Identity**: National ID, Languages, and Disability declarations.
- **Phase 2: Education**: Historical educational nodes.
- **Phase 3: Endorsements**: 3 professional referees (Required for RDB nodes).
- **Phase 4: Artifacts**: CV / Portfolio upload.

## 2. Activity Monitoring (`/jobseeker/dashboard`)
- **Status Dashboard**: Track the real-time status of your mission applications (Applied, Shortlisted, Selected).
- **AI Skill Radar**: Benchmarking tool for your skills against the Rwanda AI ecosystem.

## 3. Session Management
Logout and Login nodes are fully functional and secure, ensuring your identity data remains protected within the Umurava AI cloud.
